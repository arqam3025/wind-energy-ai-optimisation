# Research-grade repository audit

Audit baseline: 29 October 2026, before the separately versioned improvement experiments. This is a technical audit performed in the same research workspace, not external peer review or an independent-personnel replication. Findings distinguish confirmed defects, confirmed limitations, and unresolved provenance risks. New experiments must retain this distinction.

**The saved controller arithmetic is reproducible, but the original 49-block study is retrospective and does not demonstrate aggregate ramp mitigation against uncontrolled wind. A newly identified calibration/development target boundary also prevents treating the first development episode's static calibration as strictly causal.** These facts constrain claims; they do not authorise changing the freeze or replacing the original results.

## New calibration/development boundary evidence

The final calibration origin is **15 October 2018 10:50**. Development episode 1 begins at **11:00**. Calibration target eligibility must be tested as `origin + horizon < decision`, including equality exclusion. A split that compares origins alone does not establish this condition.

| Horizon | Last calibration target | Calibration residuals unavailable at first development decision | Shared calibration target pairs with development's scored h1 observations |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 10 min | 11:00 | 1 | 0 |
| 20 min | 11:10 | 2 | 1 |
| 30 min | 11:20 | 3 | 2 |
| 40 min | 11:30 | 4 | 3 |
| 50 min | 11:40 | 5 | 4 |
| 60 min | 11:50 | 6 | 5 |
| Total | | **21** | **15** |

The shared observations are 11:10, 11:20, 11:30, 11:40 and 11:50, counted across different forecast horizons. There is **no same-horizon calibration/development target intersection** because origins remain disjoint; the problem is shared observations across horizons and non-observable calibration targets at initial decisions. A horizon-h fixed calibration remains causally ineligible for the first h development decisions, giving 21 affected decision/horizon cells. The first-decision unavailable-residual count is also 21; these are distinct counts that happen to coincide.

A read-only sensitivity calculation purges each horizon's targets equal to or after the first decision and recomputes the same finite-sample absolute-error order statistic. Horizons 1–5 give identical margins. Horizon 6 changes from 786.612837729493 to 786.827977441407 kW, a **+0.215140 kW** change. This quantifies the immediate margin effect; it does not re-solve episode 1 or establish that controller selection would change. No saved quantile or original trace is modified.

This defect concerns the earliest development episode's fixed calibration, not a newly found runtime leak in November–December evaluation. All these calibration targets precede the evaluation. The adaptive module's strict filter at `scripts/causal_one_sided_uncertainty.py:123` excludes equal/future targets even though full history is preloaded. Preloading an immutable history and filtering by correct target time is not itself leakage. Its hard-coded floors' derivation remains a separate unresolved provenance issue.

Raw SCADA contains 57 negative measured power values, while the retained historical feature matrix includes 56 negative current-power features. Those source features preserve the measurement convention; clipped nonnegative physical targets/exports in the controller evaluation use a different, explicitly physical convention. This is not evidence of future leakage. New forecast diagnostics must state whether their target is the raw measurement or clipped available wind and compare every baseline against the same target.

## Frozen physical and numerical findings

The frozen study uses capacity 2,000 kWh, power 1,000 kW, energy 800–1,800 kWh (40–90% SOC), initial/reference energy 1,000 kWh, charge/discharge efficiencies 0.95, step 1/6 h, six-step horizon and ramp threshold 200 kW per ten-minute interval. The 1,000 kWh reserve is soft. Requested first actions are shared across nominal/downside scenarios; actual wind limits charging and then curtailment, with physical discharge clipping. There is no grid charging or grid-export upper cap.

Two optimisation priorities are solved, distinct from the six prediction steps. Primary minimises weighted predicted exceedance. Secondary may spend a **100 kW aggregate horizon allowance** and trades absolute grid/battery ramps, exceedance, AC-side throughput, terminal reference deviation, curtailment and reserve shortfall. It is not a strict primary optimum guarantee and supplies no realised grid-compliance guarantee. Only the first action is executed before replanning.

| AC-side battery throughput (kWh) | 35,486.897869 | 25,598.676938 |

Adaptive therefore has 27.8644% less throughput, 0.7726% less summed exceedance and sixteen fewer violations than fixed. Its actual exported energy is 0.987701 MWh lower; its hypothetical terminal-inventory-adjusted export is 0.551229 MWh higher. These two export quantities must remain distinct. Terminal restoration is accounting, not an executed feasible dispatch schedule. Equivalent full cycles are a throughput proxy, not a measured battery lifetime or degradation result.

The previous audit checked all 98 traces, 42,336 causal decision/horizon margins, all raw horizon targets and all 14 input features, and re-solved episode 1 for both controllers with identical numeric output. This audit does not relabel that limited fresh replay as a new full 49-block solver replication.

## Implementation gates and verification plan

1. Preserve this baseline report and inventory before new experiment code. Keep frozen controller/model/data/result hashes unchanged throughout.
2. Version all new research modules/configurations/output paths. Require explicit chronology, target-causal fixed calibration and complete role-aware overlap diagnostics. Existing retrospective cohort remains available as a named legacy study, not a fresh holdout.
3. Add data-independent tests for target boundaries, unavailable history, physical execution, charge/discharge exclusivity, grid balance, terminal accounting, solver failures and resume corruption. Compare the common engine against the frozen formulation on prespecified development inputs before interpreting additional baseline results.
4. Record a fixed, manageable experiment plan on development episodes whose first decisions follow all fixed-calibration targets. Report all planned outcomes and failed runs. New retrospective benchmark runs must not select a controller or alter historical headline figures.
5. Report paired differences and chronology-aware uncertainty with declared resampling choices; do not treat horizons/ten-minute samples as independent or select interval construction by whether it excludes zero.
6. Generate figures/tables from persisted, verified run artifacts; keep actual versus adjusted export and retrospective versus development labels visible. Publish only metrics that were actually computed and solver internals that were actually logged.
7. Run appropriate tests and smoke checks; record failed/unrun checks. A clean environment and permitted artifact-access route remain necessary before claiming independent public reproduction.

No new experiment outcome is asserted in this baseline audit. Subsequent work should reference its own configuration, output provenance and test record. A methodological change may create a new explicitly labelled experiment; it must never silently repair or overwrite the original frozen study.

## Subsequent implementation record

The baseline findings above remain intact. The versioned extension in `src/windresearch/` now implements explicit hash checks under `python -O`, target-causal calibration guards, complete development-role overlap detection, exclusive output paths, failure-aware instrumented MPC, independent physical/plan validation, inference-only forecasting diagnostics and segment-aware paired intervals. Historical calibration and cohort defects are documented rather than overwritten; a separate purged projection demonstrates the boundary correction. Past evaluation exposure cannot be repaired in software.

Executed studies and numerical evidence are in [EXPERIMENTAL_RESULTS.md](EXPERIMENTAL_RESULTS.md): all 294 six-case comparison jobs, 57 development sensitivity jobs and 27 development ablation jobs completed without solver failures. The three repeated development baselines reproduce exactly across independently scheduled runs. Full-study executed sources are authenticated in `audits/research_grade_20261029/executed_sources/`; post-run changes only strengthen validation. No frozen algorithm, model, parameter, observation or original score was changed.

The independent verifier initially rejected three future oracle plan rows because its charge/discharge product threshold used kW² instead of a power tolerance. Physical overlap is at most 0.000392171 kW, within the engine's predeclared 0.001 kW constraint tolerance; these h50/h60 plans were never executed. The failed report is retained, and the revised planning check uses the existing dimensioned tolerance. Executed-action checks remain strict. This is a validation correction, not a solver/controller/result change.

Release and scientific limits remain open: genuinely untouched confirmation, floor provenance, data rights/version/clock semantics, a clean isolated-environment reproduction, manuscript reconciliation and realistic deployment/degradation evidence.
